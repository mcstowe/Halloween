02 / THE ORIGINS

WHERE THE HAUNTING BEGAN.

Before the candy, costumes, and horror marathons, there were fires, folklore, and a changing season.

01

ANCIENT ROOTS

The Gaelic festival of Samhain, observed around the end of the harvest season, is frequently connected to Halloween’s origins. It marked a transition toward winter, with traditions associated with spirits and the boundary between seasons.

02

ALL HALLOWS’ EVE

In the Christian calendar, November 1 became All Saints’ Day, also called All Hallows’ Day. Its preceding evening was known as All Hallows’ Eve — the name that eventually became Halloween.

03

THE HALLOWEEN WE KNOW

Over centuries, regional customs evolved and blended. Immigration helped bring Halloween traditions to North America, where trick-or-treating, pumpkin carving, and community celebrations became especially popular.

A little history note: Halloween has multiple influences, and historians debate how directly some modern customs descend from ancient practices.
